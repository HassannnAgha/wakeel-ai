Wakeel AI

Legal research on Pakistani case law, where every answer is traced to the paragraph it came from.

Agha Ali Hassan and Warda Fatima
FAST-NUCES Lahore

The problem

A growing backlog

2.36 million cases were pending on 31 December 2024, 83% of them in the district courts. [1]

Precedent is found by hand

Law reports (PLD, SCMR, PLJ) are searched page by page or by keyword, and keyword search misses cases worded differently.

Chatbots answer without sources

A general chatbot is not tied to Pakistani judgments and can invent a citation. In law that is worse than no answer.

[1] Law and Justice Commission of Pakistan, Judicial Statistics, July to December 2024, as reported by Minute Mirror (minutemirror.com.pk).

Proposed solution

Answers you can check

One rule runs through all of it: write only from retrieved judgment paragraphs, link every claim to its source, and say so when the judgments do not answer.

Ask in your language

English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, typed or spoken. Each claim links to the paragraph it came from.

Explain my document

Upload an FIR, notice or order for a plain-words meaning, ranked red flags and deadlines. It is never stored.

Draft and verify

Draft from your facts, then check every section against the official text and every case against the library, with no AI.

A team of agents

An orchestrator picks the agents for the job and shows each one working, from research to a final verifier.

Knowledge base: real Lahore High Court judgments and the statutes they cite, copied word for word from official sources.

Why it is built this way

Chunk by paragraph

Our first chunker discarded short paragraphs: 151 of 647, holdings included. The fixed chunker keeps all 647.

Meaning and keywords

On 5 questions about a named section, meaning-only search found the right judgment 60% of the time; keyword search, 100%.

Verify without AI

Fixed rules check each citation and fact against its source, so the checker cannot be talked into agreeing with the model.
